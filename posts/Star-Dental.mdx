**Star Dental Opens New Full-Service Dental Office in Denver, Colorado**

**Denver, CO – \[Insert Date]** – Star Dental is proud to announce the opening of its new state-of-the-art dental office located at **1490 S Sheridan Blvd, Denver, CO 80232**. The practice provides convenient, affordable, and comprehensive dental care to patients throughout Denver and Lakewood, with a strong focus on same-day emergency dentistry.

Open seven days a week, including Saturdays and Sundays, Star Dental makes high-quality care accessible to busy families and working professionals. Services include preventive checkups, cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, oral surgery, restorative treatments, and pediatric care. What sets Star Dental apart is its commitment to urgent care, offering same-day appointments and welcoming walk-ins for dental emergencies such as severe tooth pain, broken or chipped teeth, infections, and knocked-out teeth.

“Our goal is to make dentistry more convenient and stress-free for patients,” said a spokesperson for Star Dental. “With extended evening hours, weekend availability, and a wide range of services under one roof, we ensure patients can get the care they need when they need it most.”

The new Denver office features advanced technology for efficient treatment in a warm, welcoming environment. Patients can expect personalized care, clear communication, and flexible payment options designed to make dentistry affordable.

Star Dental proudly serves the Denver and Lakewood communities and is now accepting new patients.
